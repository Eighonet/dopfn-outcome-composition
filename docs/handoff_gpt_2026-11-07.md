## 5. Версии статьи

### A. Аудит контекста (рекомендуется), с главным тезисом (б)

*Рабочее название: «Where Does the Lift Come From? Context Construction for Causal Foundation Models in Uplift Modeling»*. Название можно сместить на тезис (б).

- **Controlled study.** We study how context construction affects tabular causal foundation models (CausalPFN, Do-PFN, TabPFN-T) on five public uplift datasets: global strategies (random, treatment-balanced, treatment-and-outcome-balanced) and per-query retrieval, against S/T/X-learners, under group-safe CV and a frozen holdout.
- **A rare-outcome failure mode and its repair.** On both rare-outcome datasets (Criteo 4.8%, LZD 3.4%), Do-PFN produces inverted uplift rankings at every context size. Raising the share of positive outcomes in the context repairs the sign: on Criteo it flips between 10% and 20% (Q̂ −0.051 → +0.087). Treatment-and-outcome balancing lifts holdout Q̂ by +0.094 [+0.085, +0.102]. Models without the inversion gain nothing.
- **Retrieval: the neighbourhood carries most of the ranking.** On 1.3M fresh Criteo rows, a plain outcome difference over the FM's own retrieved neighbours ranks at least as well as the FM (Q̂ 0.065 vs 0.051). The FM still adds an independent residual signal (Q̂ 0.039 [0.030, 0.048]). A tuned S-Learner remains ahead.
- **Evaluation pitfalls and a reproducible pipeline.** Duplicate-vector leakage (pending repeats), seed-conditional bootstrap, Qini–AUUC collinearity, zero context ATE under T+Y; full end-to-end reproduction on independent hardware.

### B. Новый метод (не рекомендуется)

Метод «outcome-aware context balancing». Возражения рецензента опираются на наши же данные:
- эффект привязан к одной модели;
- t-интервал по трём seed'ам включает ноль;
- метод чинит перевёрнутую базовую линию;
- Do-PFN T+Y хуже S-Learner;
- заранее зарегистрированный E17 выигрыш не подтвердил.
